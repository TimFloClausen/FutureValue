# Welcome to FutureValue!

FutureValue is a Python program that calculates how your money can grow over time using three different investment methods:

* **MSCI World**
* **S&P 500**
* **Traditional bank savings**

You can choose between a **one-time investment** and **monthly contributions**, set your investment period in years, and see how much your money could grow through the power of **compound interest**.

Explore different investment scenarios and see how your savings could develop over time.

**Press Enter to start!**
